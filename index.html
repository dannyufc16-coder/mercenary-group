Mercenary Operational Command

ALL IN ONE AND ONE FOR ALL
Chain of Command
Rank / Role Designation Authority & Responsibilities
Commander-in-Chief Dannyuf 16 Absolute authority. Sets high-level strategy, approves high-value contracts, and commands all operations.
Vice President Msromero Executive second-in-command. Manages daily logistics, oversees field operations, and steps in during Commander absence.
Vanguard (Rank 3) Field Elite Directly answers to executive leadership. Leads high-risk strikes, fort sieges, and tactical squads.
Enforcer (Rank 2) Full Operative Authorized for independent combat, gang hideout raids, and convoy defense.
Contractor (Rank 1) Recruit New hire under evaluation. Restricted to guard duty and basic bounties under supervision.
Progression & Operations

Step 1: Recruitment & Evaluation (Supervised by Msromero)

Objective: Prove basic survival, combat competence, and weapon handling under pressure.

Gear Restriction: Standard sidearms only (e.g., Cattleman Revolver), regular ammo, basic clothing.

  • Marksmanship Test: Land 5 consecutive headshots on hostile NPCs without critical damage.
  • Hunting Assessment: Retrieve 3 pristine (3-star) pelts or carcasses within a single in-game day.
  • Bounty Extraction: Capture a target alive and transport them to drop-off without dying.
  • Promotion: Complete all tasks for approval from Msromero to reach Contractor.

Step 2: Field Contracts & Convoy Security

Objective: Execute tactical operations, manage resources, and coordinate squad combat.

Gear Restriction: Unlocks Repeaters, Shotguns, high-velocity ammo, and throwing weapons.

  • Caravan Escort: Defend high-value cargo across territories (e.g., Valentine to Saint Denis).
  • Gang Hideout Clearance: Infiltrate and clear heavy enemy strongholds (e.g., Hanging Dog Ranch).
  • Asset Retrieval: Secure stolen equipment/vehicles under active enemy fire.
  • Promotion: Complete 5 field contracts with zero failures to reach Enforcer.

Step 3: High-Value Operations & Territorial Control (Dannyuf 16 Directives)

Objective: Execute strategic strikes planned directly by executive command.

Gear Restriction: Full access to high-tier weaponry (Sniper Rifles, Dynamite, Fire Bottles) and custom uniforms.

  • The Fort Siege: Assault and control Fort Wallace or Bolger Glade for 5 minutes against enemy waves.
  • High-Target Elimination: Neutralize heavily guarded urban targets without early law alert.
  • Armored Transport Robbery: Intercept high-security supply trains and execute a clean withdrawal.
  • Promotion: Lead 3 successful executive operations to earn Vanguard rank.
Command Protocol & Rules
  • Executive Veto: Major payouts and strategic selections require final sign-off from Dannyuf 16 or Msromero.
  • Medical Leave Penalty: Failing a contract results in a 1 in-game day suspension or 20% cut reduction on next job.
  • Payout Structure: Treasury (30%), Vanguards (30%), Enforcers (25%), Contractors (15%).
